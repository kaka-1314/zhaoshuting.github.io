🔬 Research Interests
---------------------

My research focuses on human motion tracking, 3D reconstruction, and depth estimation. I am currently working on AI for healthcare and human body shape estimation. Specifically, I am interested in:

<strong style="color: #3699c8;">AI for Health:</strong> Developing deep learning methods for health assessment, rehabilitation, and clinical decision support using multimodal physiological, behavioral, and medical data.

<strong style="color: #3699c8;">Human Motion Analysis:</strong> Building models for 3D human motion tracking, body shape estimation, and motion generation from multimodal signals such as images and wearable sensors, with applications in AR/VR and human-machine interaction.

<strong style="color: #3699c8;">Robotics and Embodied AI for Health:</strong> Learning embodied perception and control policies for healthcare and assistive robotics that understand human intent and support physical interaction.
